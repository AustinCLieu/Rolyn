General rules

These rules apply to everyone on Rolyn — whether you're posting a job, offering a service, or browsing listings.

  • Be honest. Only post real opportunities and genuine services. Misleading titles, fake job offers, and bait-and-switch listings are strictly prohibited.
  • Treat people with respect. Harassment, threats, hate speech, and discriminatory language of any kind will result in immediate removal and a permanent ban.
  • One post per listing. Do not post the same job or service multiple times across categories. Duplicate posts will be removed.
  • Use your real identity. Rolyn is a community built on trust. Impersonating another person or organisation is a serious violation.
  • Keep it legal. Any listing that involves illegal activity — including unlicensed work, unreported income schemes, or exploitation — will be removed and reported to authorities where appropriate.

Personal safety

Meeting strangers carries risk. Follow these practices every time you arrange a meeting through Rolyn.

Always tell someone where you're going before meeting a Rolyn contact for the first time. Share the listing, the address, and the person's contact details with a friend or family member.
  • Meet in public first. For initial meetings — especially for jobs that involve entering someone's home — meet in a café, barangay hall, or other public location before agreeing to work.
  • Bring a companion. You are always allowed to bring a friend or family member to a first meeting or initial job visit. Any employer who refuses this should be considered a red flag.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong — a too-good-to-be-true offer, unusual questions, or pressure to decide immediately — walk away and report the listing.
  • Do not share sensitive documents upfront. Never send photos of your passport, government ID, or bank account details before you have verified the employer's identity in person.
  • Verify the address. Before a home visit, confirm the address exists and matches what the poster provided. A quick map search can reveal inconsistencies.
  • Keep communication on-platform. Use Rolyn messaging as long as possible before moving to personal numbers. This preserves a record if a dispute arises.

Payments & scams

Payment fraud is the most common type of scam on community job boards. Learn to recognise the warning signs.

Rolyn will never ask you to pay a fee to apply for a job, unlock a listing, or receive a payment. Any message claiming otherwise is a scam.
  • Never pay to get hired. Legitimate employers do not charge application fees, "registration fees", uniform deposits, or training costs before you start work. These are scams.
  • Be wary of overpayment. A classic scam involves an employer sending a cheque or transfer for more than the agreed amount, then asking you to return the difference. The original payment is always fake.
  • Do not accept gift cards as payment. No legitimate employer pays wages in GCash vouchers, Lazada credits, or iTunes gift cards. If asked, report immediately.
  • Confirm payment terms in writing. Before starting any job, agree on the rate, payment schedule, and method in writing — a message thread is sufficient.
  • Use traceable payment methods. Bank transfers and GCash provide a record. Cash is fine for small one-off jobs but avoid it for larger, ongoing arrangements.
  • Verify wire transfer requests. If you are a hirer, independently verify any new bank account before sending payment. Call the worker on a number you sourced yourself, not one provided in the transfer request.

Prohibited content

The following types of listings and messages are not permitted on Rolyn under any circumstances.

Illegal services

Any service or job that involves unlicensed activity, smuggling, illegal substances, or violations of Philippine labour law.

Exploitation

Job offers that involve deceptive recruitment, unsafe working conditions, or compensation below minimum wage.

Adult content

Listings of an explicit or sexual nature are strictly banned. This includes coded or implied solicitation.

Pyramid schemes

Multi-level marketing recruitment posts, chain letters, and any scheme where earnings depend primarily on recruiting others.

Hate speech

Content that demeans individuals or groups based on race, religion, gender, age, disability, or sexual orientation.

Spam & fake listings

Auto-generated posts, listings with fabricated details, or posts designed to harvest contact information.

Posting rules

Good listings help everyone — they get filled faster and attract more serious applicants. Follow these standards when creating a post.

  • Be specific about the job. Include location, schedule, required skills, and a realistic pay range. Vague posts attract poor matches and waste everyone's time.
  • Post in the correct category. Choose the category that best describes the role — cleaning, cooking, childcare, transportation, or general service. Misplaced posts may be moved or removed.
  • Keep pay ranges honest. Do not advertise an inflated pay range to attract applicants and then offer less. This is considered deceptive and violates our guidelines.
  • Update or remove filled listings promptly. Once a position is filled, mark it as closed or delete it. Leaving filled listings live wastes applicants' time.
  • No third-party recruitment. Listings must be posted by the direct employer or the person offering the service — not by recruitment agencies acting without disclosure.
  • Images must be relevant. Any photos attached to a listing must accurately represent the job or service. Stock photos, misleading images, or unrelated photos are not permitted.

Privacy

Protecting your personal information — and that of others — is a shared responsibility on Rolyn.

  • Do not share others' personal information. Posting someone else's address, contact number, or photos without their consent is a violation of their privacy and may be illegal.
  • Limit what you share in public listings. Your full home address, personal mobile number, and financial information should only be shared privately, after initial contact is established.
  • Be cautious with document requests. Employers may legitimately need to verify your identity before hiring, but this should happen in person — not by sending scanned IDs over chat to an unverified contact.
  • Rolyn does not sell your data. Your information is used only to operate the platform. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Reporting & enforcement

You are our most important line of defence. If you see something that doesn't look right, please report it.

Reports are confidential. The person you report will never be told who flagged them. Our safety team reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • Use the report button. Every listing and message on Rolyn has a report option. Use it for spam, suspicious behaviour, prohibited content, or anything that makes you uncomfortable.
  • Violations have consequences. Depending on severity, violations may result in a warning, temporary suspension, permanent ban, or referral to law enforcement.
  • Repeated minor violations are treated seriously. Even small infractions — like duplicate postings or vague job descriptions — accumulate. Repeat offenders face suspension.
  • False reports are also a violation. Reporting someone as retaliation or to gain a competitive advantage is prohibited and may result in your own account being sanctioned.
  • Emergency situations. If you are in immediate danger, contact the Philippine National Police (PNP) at 117 or the emergency hotline 911. Do not wait to file a Rolyn report first.
